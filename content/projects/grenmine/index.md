GRENMINE is a research project funded by the European Commission under the Research Fund for
Coal and Steel, running from July 2024 to June 2027. An international consortium coordinated by
Wrocław University of Science and Technology develops gravitational energy storage in
post-mining areas: heavy masses moved between different heights work like a battery, charged
when electricity is cheap and discharged when the demand is high.

<!--more-->

TerraEye takes part as a supplier of software and data for the calculations of the other
partners. It prepared digital terrain models of the Polish mine pits, DEM Profiler, a tool that
calculates the geometry of slopes, and a harmonised database of mine locations from several
European countries.
