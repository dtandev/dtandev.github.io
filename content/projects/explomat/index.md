EXPLOMAT (Sustainable Exploration Technologies for Efficient Raw Materials Discovery) is an
innovation project awarded by EIT RawMaterials under Horizon Europe, in its call for regional
innovation. It runs for two years, from June 2025 to May 2027. TerraEye is the lead partner: it
develops the technology, manages the project and prepares the product for the market.

<!--more-->

The aim is a set of AI tools, built on Earth Observation data, that make mineral exploration
faster, cheaper and less invasive than manual field surveys and interpretation that depends on
the expert. TerraEye works on three components: automatic detection of mineral outcrops in
multispectral satellite images, a model that finds stress in vegetation caused by metal-rich
soils, which allows exploring forested areas without clearing them, and semi-automated analysis
of geological structures from satellite and terrain data.

In the second phase of the project the tools are to be validated in a working bauxite mine in
Bosnia and Herzegovina, which is the first test of the product in an industrial setting.
