RISPECT is a research and innovation project co-funded by EIT RawMaterials, running from
February 2025 to the end of 2026. It develops an integrated tool for efficient, low-impact
exploration of critical raw materials, based on synthetic hyperspectral imagery,
ground-penetrating radar and an AI platform that combines them.

<!--more-->

TerraEye works on the satellite part: synthetic hyperspectral imagery generated from satellite
images, which is meant to point to the places of mineral deposits more precisely. Its partners
are WIDMO from Poland, which develops the ground-penetrating radar, and SAISLAB from Greece,
which leads the AI platform.
