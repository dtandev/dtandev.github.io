abstract: >-
  Accurate mineral identification from hyperspectral satellite data is critical for efficient
  mineral exploration. Traditional one-class classification methods, such as OCSVM and Isolation
  Forest with data augmentation, often struggle to generalize across diverse spectral patterns,
  leading to low F1 scores despite high recall. In this study, we propose a metric learning
  network approach for a one-class mineral classification, utilizing an augmented dataset and
  preprocessing techniques such as continuum removal. The results show that the proposed method
  significantly improves the F1 scores for minerals such as Alunite or Jarosite compared to
  previous methods, while using continuous removal further enhances recall. These findings
  demonstrate that a simple metric learning architecture, combined with careful preprocessing, can
  outperform traditional one-class classifiers and provide more reliable detection of target
  minerals from hyperspectral data. The proposed solution achieves F1-scores up to 0.725
  (Jarosite) and 0.451 (Alunite), representing multiple-fold improvements over previous one-class
  baselines, with recall reaching 0.945 and 0.679 respectively.
