abstract: >-
  This monograph presents a GeoAI methodology for forecasting changes in agrarian structure,
  combining land and building registry (EGiB), real estate price register (RCN), and local
  zoning plan (MPZP) data on a regular hexagonal grid, on which a LightGBM model is trained to
  identify areas at risk of land parcelling or de-agriculturalisation. The methodology is
  validated on a case study of Ostróda County, chosen for its strong investment pressure and
  diverse land-use patterns. The book closes with recommendations for local government
  administration on using such models in spatial planning. The book is written in Polish,
  original title: "Sztuczna inteligencja w ocenie kierunków zmian struktury agrarnej".
