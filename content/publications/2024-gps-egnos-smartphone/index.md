abstract: >-
  The potential for the use of smartphones in GNSSs (Global Navigation Satellite Systems)
  positioning has increased in recent years due to the emergence of the ability of Android-based
  devices used to process raw satellite data. This paper presents the results of a study on the
  use of SBAS data transmitted by the EGNOS (European Geostationary Navigation Overlay Service)
  system in GNSS positioning using a Xiaomi Mi8 smartphone. Raw data recorded at a fixed point
  were used in post-processing calculations in GPS/EGNOS positioning by determining the
  coordinates for every second of a session of about 5 h and comparing the results to those
  obtained with a Septentrio AsteRx2 GNSS receiver operating at the same time at a distance of
  about 3 m. The calculations were performed using the assumptions of the GNSS/SBAS positioning
  algorithms, which were modified with carrier-phase smoothed code observations and the content of
  the corrections transmitted by EGNOS.
