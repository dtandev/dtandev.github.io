abstract: >-
  Spectral similarity measures such as the Spectral Angle Mapper (SAM) treat all spectral bands
  equally, an assumption difficult to justify for modern imaging spectroscopy data where
  measurement uncertainty varies strongly with wavelength. We introduce wSAM, an uncertainty-
  weighted extension of SAM that incorporates per-band observational uncertainty as inverse-
  variance weights. On 47 EMIT–RadCalNet observation pairs across five sites, wSAM (w= 1/σ2)
  reduces mean spectral angle by 49% relative to SAM and outperforms both hard band masking and
  alternative weighting schemes, while a cross-site discriminability analysis confirms that
  weighting sharpens rather than erases spectral differences between surface types.
